Referral Marketing Disclosure

We think you should know how this website operates and how the people who do your water damage cleanup work are paid. This page explains it in plain language.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

How This Site Works

water-damage-cleanup-houston.com is a marketing website for Water Damage Cleanup Houston. When you submit the estimate form or call a number on this site, your request comes to us.

Depending on the type of work, where the property is, and how the schedule looks, a project may be handled by our own team or referred to an independent, qualified contractor we work with in Houston. When a referral leads to work, we may receive compensation from that contractor.

We also use call tracking and analytics so we can tell which pages and advertising sources produce inquiries. That is measurement, not a change to what you are quoted.

What This Means for You

  • You will know who is coming. Ask who will perform the work and we will tell you before anyone is scheduled.
  • Your written estimate is the price. Whatever arrangement exists behind the scenes, the signed estimate is what you pay.
  • You are free to compare. Getting a second opinion on a water damage cleanup project is reasonable, and we will not push you away from doing it.
  • Independent contractors are independent. They carry their own licensing, insurance, and warranty terms. Ask to see them, and read the agreement you sign with them.

Advertising and third parties

This site may appear in paid search results or other advertising. Any third party service we use to run or measure that advertising receives only what it needs to do the job. Our Privacy Policy covers what is collected and shared.

Questions About a Referral

If you want to know who would handle your project, whether a referral is involved, or how to reach a contractor directly, just ask. We would rather answer the question up front than have you wonder about it later.

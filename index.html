What People Ask Us

What should I do first when I discover water damage?

If it is safe, stop the water source and keep people away from affected electrical equipment. Avoid entering rooms with standing water when electrical hazards may be present. Contact a water damage cleanup professional and explain how the water entered the property.

How quickly should water damage cleanup begin?

Cleanup should begin as soon as conditions are safe and the water source has been controlled. Moisture can move into drywall, flooring, insulation, and other materials, making later cleanup more complicated. The amount of water, its source, and the affected materials determine the urgency.

Can water damage lead to mold in Houston?

Yes. Houston's warm, humid conditions can make damp indoor materials more difficult to dry. Mold growth may begin within 24 to 48 hours under favorable conditions, so identifying and addressing moisture promptly is important.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage cleanup?

Coverage depends on the policy, the cause of the damage, and any applicable exclusions or endorsements. Sudden plumbing failures may be handled differently from rising floodwater or long-term leaks. Your insurance provider is the best source for confirming coverage.

Do you help with water damage in areas outside central Houston?

Our service area includes Meyerland, Memorial, Kingwood, Spring Branch, Braeswood, and Westbury, along with Houston. Contact us with your property location and a description of the damage so we can discuss service arrangements.

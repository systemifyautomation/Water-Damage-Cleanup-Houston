Floors, Walls, and Cabinets

Water can move beneath laminate, vinyl, wood, or other flooring materials. It may also collect behind baseboards or soak into cabinet bases and drywall. These areas can remain damp after visible water has been removed.

Houston's climate can make lingering moisture a concern. Drying may require more than opening a window or running a household fan. In some situations, controlled air movement and dehumidification are appropriate.

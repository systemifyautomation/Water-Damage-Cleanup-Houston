Homeowner Story

Reginald's Story

A retired Katy homeowner returned from a family visit to find his water heater had failed.

This is Reginald's story.

“I'm 67 years old, retired, and I've been living in Katy, Texas, for almost 23 years. I raised my children in this house, and even though they've grown up and moved away, I've never considered leaving. This is where I feel at home.

Last summer, I spent a few days visiting my daughter and grandchildren. When I returned, I noticed an unusual damp smell as soon as I opened the front door.

I walked down the hallway and discovered that my water heater had failed while I was away. Water had spread from the utility room into my bedroom and living room. The wooden flooring was buckling, the carpet was soaked, and the bottom of the walls was visibly damaged.

I've always been a hands-on person. I spent most of my life fixing things myself, but this was something I simply couldn't handle alone. I sat down on my front porch, trying to figure out what to do next. I felt frustrated, helpless, and worried about how much of my home I was going to lose.

My daughter helped me find a local water damage restoration company. When the crew arrived, they inspected the affected rooms and discovered moisture trapped underneath the flooring and behind the walls.

They removed the damaged materials, extracted the remaining water, and brought in industrial fans and dehumidifiers. They checked on the drying process regularly and explained what they were doing along the way.

Once everything was dry, they repaired the damaged walls, replaced the flooring, and restored the affected rooms.

What I appreciated was that they didn't try to turn my house into something new. They respected what was already there and focused on fixing what had been damaged.

When I finally moved back in, I made myself a cup of coffee and sat in my favorite chair by the window. It was the first time in weeks that I felt comfortable again.

At this stage of my life, I wasn't looking for a new house or a fresh start. I just wanted the home I'd spent 23 years building memories in. And that's what I got back.”

Homeowner Story

Amina's Story

After a night of heavy rain, a Meyerland family found their home covered in floodwater and mud.

This is Amina's story.

“I've lived in Meyerland, Houston, Texas, with my husband and two daughters for 12 years. I never imagined that a single night of heavy rain could destroy so much of what we had worked for.

When we returned home after the flood, everything was covered in mud. Our furniture was soaked, the kitchen cabinets were damaged, and there were watermarks along almost every wall. Seeing my daughters' toys and childhood belongings ruined was particularly heartbreaking.

I felt helpless. I didn't know where to begin, what could be saved, or how long it would take before we could live in our house again.

When the restoration crew arrived, they explained everything to us. They removed the contaminated water, cleaned the affected rooms, removed damaged drywall, and installed professional drying equipment. They kept us informed throughout the entire process, which gave us some much-needed peace of mind.

Over the following weeks, they repaired our walls, replaced the damaged flooring, and restored our kitchen and bedrooms.

I'll never forget walking through our front door and finally feeling like I was home again. After weeks of uncertainty, stress, and sleepless nights, we could finally get back to our normal lives.

You don't realize how much the comfort of your own home means to you until you lose it. Getting it back was an incredible feeling.”

Careers

Water Damage Cleanup Houston is interested in people who enjoy hands-on work, communicate clearly, and take property care seriously. Water damage cleanup can involve physical work, changing conditions, and direct contact with people dealing with stressful situations. We value dependable team members who are willing to learn, follow safety procedures, and treat customers respectfully. Roles listed here describe potential opportunities and do not guarantee a current vacancy or specific employment terms. For employment inquiries, email service@water-damage-cleanup-houston.com.

What We Look For

  • Care about the work. We want people who take pride in doing water damage cleanup properly, not just quickly.
  • Straight talk with customers. You will be in someone's home or business explaining what you found. Plain language beats jargon.
  • Reliability. Showing up when you said you would matters more here than any single line on a resume.
  • Respect for the property. Protecting the space, cleaning up, and leaving it better than you found it.
  • Safety, every time. Following safe practice is part of the job, not an extra.

Experience in water damage cleanup or a related trade helps. If you have the attitude and not yet the trade experience, say so, because we do train.

Roles We Hire For

We are a small team, so openings come and go with the work. These are the roles we hire into.

Water Damage Cleanup Technician

Help with water removal, affected-area preparation, equipment setup, cleaning, and basic documentation under appropriate supervision. Technicians work in residential and commercial properties where the type and extent of damage can vary considerably. This role requires attention to safety, careful handling of materials, and a willingness to explain work respectfully when interacting with customers.

Qualifications

  • Ability to perform physical work, including moving appropriate equipment and materials.
  • Willingness to follow safety instructions and use required protective equipment.
  • Dependable attendance and the ability to work as part of a field team.
  • Basic communication skills and a respectful approach to customers.
  • Experience in property maintenance, cleaning, construction, or restoration is helpful but not always essential.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to learn practical water damage cleanup skills.
  • Hands-on experience with varied property conditions and materials.
  • Team-oriented work with opportunities for skill development.
  • Specific pay, scheduling, training, and benefit details discussed for any available position.

Water Damage Project Coordinator

Support the organization of cleanup projects by gathering job information, coordinating field activities, communicating with property owners, and maintaining clear project records. This role suits someone who can manage multiple details while keeping communication calm and understandable.

Qualifications

  • Strong organizational and customer communication skills.
  • Comfort keeping records and tracking project information.
  • Ability to coordinate tasks and respond to changing priorities.
  • Basic familiarity with property maintenance or restoration work is helpful.
  • Attention to detail and a practical problem-solving approach.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop coordination and customer service experience.
  • Exposure to residential and commercial cleanup projects.
  • Collaborative work with field personnel.
  • Role-specific compensation and employment benefits discussed when a position is offered.

Moisture Assessment Technician

Assist with evaluating water-affected building materials, recording moisture conditions, and supporting equipment planning and drying observations. This position calls for careful documentation and an interest in understanding how water behaves in different parts of a building.

Qualifications

  • Interest in building materials, moisture behavior, and drying methods.
  • Ability to record observations accurately and follow procedures.
  • Comfort working in changing residential and commercial environments.
  • Awareness of workplace safety and the limitations of available equipment.
  • Relevant field experience or training is helpful, with role requirements confirmed during hiring.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to build specialized knowledge of moisture-related property damage.
  • Experience working with different building materials and layouts.
  • Potential for additional responsibilities as skills develop.
  • Training arrangements, pay, and benefits confirmed for any available position.

Customer Service and Scheduling Assistant

Help respond to customer inquiries, collect the basic details of water damage situations, organize contact information, and support communication between customers and field personnel. The position is suited to someone who can remain calm, listen carefully, and explain the next steps without making unsupported promises.

Qualifications

  • Clear written and spoken communication.
  • Good listening skills and a courteous manner.
  • Ability to organize messages, appointments, and basic records.
  • Comfort handling urgent inquiries and changing priorities.
  • Experience in customer service or office coordination is helpful.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop administrative and customer service skills.
  • Exposure to the operations of a Houston service-area business.
  • Collaborative contact with field and coordination personnel.
  • Employment terms and available benefits explained during the hiring process.

How to Apply

Email service@water-damage-cleanup-houston.com with "Careers" and the role in the subject line. Include:

  • A short note about the kind of work you have done
  • Your resume, or simply a list of previous jobs and dates
  • The area you can reliably get to
  • A phone number and the best time to reach you

We read every application. If there is a fit, expect a short phone conversation first, then a meeting and a ride-along on a job so you can see the work before you commit to it.

We do not always have an opening posted. Send your information anyway. We keep applications on file and reach out first to people who already introduced themselves when a spot opens.

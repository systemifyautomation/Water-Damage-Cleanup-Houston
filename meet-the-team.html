How We Work Together

Our work begins with listening. We ask where the water came from, what happened before it was discovered, and which areas are affected. From there, we consider safety, accessible water, moisture in surrounding materials, and any contamination concerns. We explain the proposed cleanup steps and why they matter before moving forward with an agreed scope.

We also believe the work should be organized around realistic expectations. Water removal, drying, contaminated material cleanup, and building repairs are not interchangeable tasks. Some projects involve only a few areas, while others need additional specialists or extensive repairs. We try to keep the process understandable and provide clear information about what has been completed and what may still be needed.
